Privacy Policy

Rufi & the Curious Forest

Effective date: 2026-11-15

In short

Rufi & the Curious Forest is a game for children aged 2–6, made by Acorn Trail Studio.

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It does not connect to the internet and asks Android for no permissions. There are no ads, in-app purchases, analytics, crash reporting tools, accounts, chat or links out of the app.

What stays on the device

To work, the game keeps the following only on this device, in the app's own private folder:

  • The child's name or nickname entered by a parent, their age and the chosen forest friend.
  • Game progress, earned toys and stickers, and the treehouse layout.
  • Settings such as sound, accessibility and daily play time.
  • A small error log written if the app misbehaves. It never leaves the device either.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else; we cannot access it. Android backup is turned off for this app, so it is not copied into cloud backups.

Deleting information

In the Parent Area you can delete a child profile, or remove everything with "Delete all data" in Settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of it from the device.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal data from children, no data reaches us under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including Article 8) or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

The Parent Area sits behind a grown-up check. Settings and deletion are only available there.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party service or software development kit that collects data. The open-source libraries it uses run on the device only.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text arrives with an app update and the effective date is updated.
